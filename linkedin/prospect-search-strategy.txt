Q-MOL LINKEDIN PROSPECT SEARCH STRATEGY
========================================

GOAL: Find 20-30 qualified leads per week in drug discovery / cheminformatics

--- SEARCH QUERIES TO USE ON LINKEDIN ---

1. "cheminformatics" + "drug discovery" + United States/UK/Germany
2. "computational chemistry" + "virtual screening" + biotech
3. "molecular modeling" + "CRO" + "medicinal chemistry"
4. "bioinformatics" + "target identification" + pharma
5. "compound library" + "screening" + "ML" + startup
6. "RDKit" + "Python" + "drug design" + researcher
7. "ADMET" + "QSAR" + "pharmaceutical" + scientist
8. "docking" + "molecular dynamics" + "biotech"
9. "early stage drug discovery" + "founder" + "CEO"
10. "AI drug discovery" + "machine learning" + "molecules"

--- FILTERS TO APPLY ---

- Connection: 2nd & 3rd (not 1st, you already know them)
- Locations: US (Boston, SF, San Diego, NJ), UK (Cambridge, London), Germany (Munich, Heidelberg), Switzerland (Basel)
- Industries: Biotechnology, Pharmaceuticals, Research, Higher Education
- Company size: 1-50 (startups most likely to buy), 51-200 (CROs), 201-500 (mid-size pharma)
- Job titles: Scientist, Senior Scientist, Principal Scientist, Director, Founder, CTO, Head of Chemistry, Cheminformatician, Computational Chemist, Medicinal Chemist, Data Scientist, ML Engineer

--- HIGH-VALUE TARGETS ---

STARTUP FOUNDERS (most likely to buy quickly):
- AI drug discovery startups (1-20 employees)
- Virtual screening companies
- CROs offering computational chemistry services
- Academic spinouts with SBIR/STTR funding

CROs & BIOTECH (need compound libraries):
- Contract research organizations doing hit identification
- Biotechs with early-stage pipelines (preclinical)
- Peptide/small molecule companies

ACADEMIC LABS (good for free trials, word of mouth):
- Professors with active NIH/NSF grants in computational chemistry
- Graduate students working on virtual screening projects
- Postdocs building ML models for drug discovery

--- OUTREACH CADENCE ---

DAY 1: Send connection request with NO message (just the default LinkedIn connection note)
DAY 3: Send personalized value-first message (Template 5 from outreach-templates.txt)
DAY 7: If no response, send follow-up with free dataset offer (Template 3)
DAY 14: If still no response, soft close — "No pressure, just wanted to make sure you saw this"

--- DAILY LIMITS (to avoid LinkedIn restrictions) ---

- Connection requests: 15-20 per day max
- Messages: 30-40 per day max
- Profile views: 50-70 per day max
- Wait at least 30 seconds between any action
- Spread activity throughout the day (not all at once)

--- TRACKING ---

Use a simple spreadsheet to track:
- Name | Company | Title | Date Connected | Message Sent | Response | Status
- Status: Connected → Messaged → Responded → Interested → Purchased → Closed
