## Recommended Email Sequences

### Academic Outreach (1 of 2)
- **Day 1:** Intro email offering free 50-molecule preview
- **Day 4:** Follow-up if no reply ("Just checking if the dataset preview was useful")
- **Day 14:** Final follow-up with a direct download link to the marketplace

### CRO Outreach (1 of 2)
- **Day 1:** LinkedIn connection request with personalized note (NOT a commercial template)
- **Day 3:** LinkedIn InMail or reply to accepted connection with 2-sentence pitch
- **Day 7:** Email via contact form with one-pager if no response

## Compliance Checklist Before Sending Any Email
- [ ] Using institutional email (not Gmail/Yahoo for business outreach)
- [ ] Physical mailing address included in signature
- [ ] Clear unsubscribe instruction included
- [ ] Subject line is truthful (not "Re: Your paper")
- [ ] No more than 1 follow-up per recipient
- [ ] Each email is individually addressed (not BCC mass mail)
