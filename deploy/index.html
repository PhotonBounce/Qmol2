What Q-Mol Can Do

🧪

50+ Molecular Descriptors

MW, logP, TPSA, QED, Lipinski rules, aromaticity, ring counts, and more. Powered by RDKit.

🤖

ML ADMET Predictions

Validated models: solubility (logS, R²=0.892), hERG inhibition (R²=0.800), BBB penetration, CYP450.

🎯

Drug-Target Screening

Screen against EGFR, AChE, BACE1. Predict binding affinity with confidence intervals.

✨

De Novo Design

Generate novel molecules from seed scaffolds. Optimize leads with genetic algorithms.

🔍

Similarity Search

Find molecules by Tanimoto similarity with ECFP4 fingerprints. Search PubChem on demand.

3D Dashboard

Interactive React dashboard with 3D molecular viewer (NGL), charts, and export to CSV/Excel.
