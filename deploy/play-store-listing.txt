Q-Mol is a molecular informatics platform for drug discovery researchers, cheminformaticians, and computational chemistry teams.

MINE MOLECULES
• Auto-harvest drug-like molecules from PubChem
• Full RDKit-validated descriptors: MW, LogP, TPSA, HBD, HBA, rotatable bonds
• QED (Quantitative Estimate of Drug-likeness) scoring
• Lipinski and Veber filter flags
• PAINS (Pan-Assay Interference Compounds) screening

FILTER & EXPORT
• Filter by QED score, molecular weight, LogP, TPSA
• Lipinski pass/fail highlighting
• PAINS-free compound filtering
• Export datasets as CSV for docking, ML, or assay preparation

DATASET MARKETPLACE
• Buy curated molecular datasets with crypto (ETH, BTC, SOL, and more)
• Instant CSV download after payment confirmation
• Free 7-day trial for registered users

WHO IS IT FOR?
• Cheminformatics scientists building compound libraries

• ML researchers training drug-discovery models
• Academic labs needing drug-like molecule sets
• CROs offering computational chemistry services

Q-Mol is actively developed by Photon Bounce — a solo studio building web apps, AI agents, and scientific tools.

AAB BUILD INSTRUCTIONS
----------------------

You currently have an APK (deploy/qmol.apk) but Google Play requires AAB format.

TO BUILD A SIGNED AAB FROM YOUR FLUTTER PROJECT:

1. Open your Flutter project in Android Studio or VS Code

2. Create/update android/key.properties:
   storePassword=YOUR_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD
   keyPassword=YOUR_KEY_PASSWORD
   keyAlias=YOUR_KEY_ALIAS
   storeFile=../your_keystore.jks

3. Build the release AAB:
   cd D:\Qmol-3
   flutter build appbundle --release

4. The AAB will be at:
   build\app\outputs\bundle\release\app-release.aab

5. Upload this AAB to Google Play Console

IF YOU DON'T HAVE A SIGNING KEY:

1. Generate one:
   cd D:\Qmol-3\android
   keytool -genkey -v -keystore qmol-key.jks -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias qmol

2. Update android/key.properties with the new keystore details

3. Build AAB as above
