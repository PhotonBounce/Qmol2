Privacy Policy for Q-Mol

Effective date: July 5, 2026

Developer: Photon Bounce (Dmitriy Buchman)

Contact: db@photon-bounce.com

1. Information We Collect

Q-Mol collects the following information when you create an account:

  • Email address (for authentication and dataset delivery)
  • Password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)
  • Crypto wallet address (optional, for payment verification)

We do not collect: real names, phone numbers, location data, contacts, or device identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and secure your session
  • To deliver purchased datasets via email
  • To verify crypto payments for marketplace transactions
  • To track your molecule mining activity (stored locally in your account)

3. Data Storage

All data is stored on our secure server (photon-bounce.com) using SQLite databases. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Molecule datasets sold on the marketplace are anonymized and do not contain user data.

5. Crypto Payments

Crypto transactions are processed on-chain. We store only the transaction hash and wallet address you provide for payment verification. We do not have access to your private keys.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by emailing db@photon-bounce.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Q-Mol is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
